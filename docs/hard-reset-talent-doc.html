Section Breakdown · Section 03

DJs on Deck — Talent

Current-state breakdown of the talent section: the same 3D Coverflow as Section 02, populated with typographic DJ cards. This documents what exists today and flags the decisions to make before we rework it.

3D COVERFLOW (shared) TYPOGRAPHIC CARDS PLACEHOLDER DATA

01Overview

Section 03 (#talent, heading "DJs on Deck") showcases residents and past/guest talent as a horizontal 3D Coverflow. It reuses the exact same initCoverflow() engine as Section 02's party carousel — but instead of flier images, each card is a typographic DJ card (.tcard): a name, genre, EQ wave, and a role tag.

Right now it holds 10 cards — 4 residents and 6 guests (3 of them marked past) — all placeholder names and data.

--accAccent colour, cycled per card: cyan / green / magenta / amber. Drives the border, name text-shadow, wave, tag, and corner glow.
.tc-hdrKicker — // RESIDENT or // GUEST.
.tc-nameDJ name — heavy (900), offset accent text-shadow. Wraps for long names (GIOVANNI<br>SAINZ).
.tc-roleGenre · city, e.g. TECHNO · SF.
.tc-wave5 animated EQ bars in the accent colour.
.tc-footRole tag (RESIDENT / GUEST / PAST) + a two-digit index (001…010).

Card shape is aspect-ratio: 4/5 with a dark diagonal gradient and a soft accent glow in the top-right corner. There is no photo — it's purely typographic.

05Behavior & inheritance

Because it runs on the same initCoverflow() as Section 02, it automatically inherited some of our recent carousel work — and drifted out of sync in other ways:

Inherited (good)

Shared coverflow behaviour

3D spread/rotation, one-item-per-swipe trackpad nav, pointer drag, the 8px matte + green active frame on the focused card.

Drifted

Still has ‹ › arrows

We removed arrows from Section 02 but not here — nav feels inconsistent between the two carousels.

Missing

No detail view

Clicking the active DJ card does nothing — there's no terminal "profile" the way parties open a detail window.

Missing

No caption / no grouping

No readout under the stack, and residents vs guests vs past are mixed in one flat row.

06Open questions / directions

Decisions to make before rebuilding — these shape everything downstream:

Card styleKeep the typographic cards, move to photo cards (DJ portraits), or a hybrid (photo with the typographic treatment over it)?
Detail viewShould clicking a DJ open a profile — like the party terminal window — with bio, genres, socials/mixes, and the parties they've played? Or stay a simple browse row?
GroupingSplit into Residents and Guests/Past (two rows or a filter), or keep one mixed row ordered by number?
ConsistencyMatch Section 02 — remove the arrows, add a caption/label under the active card?
DataSwap placeholders for the real roster — names, genres, links (SoundCloud / Instagram / mixes).
